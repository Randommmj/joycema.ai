2023 Buffett Annual Meeting | A Running Diary

May 8, 2023

This year, Buffett is 92 and Munger is 99.‍‍‍‍

Together with Shenshen from Heshenshi and a bunch of friends, I went to Omaha in the spirit of a pilgrimage and did every single thing a fan could possibly do.

Reading about it and being there in person really feel completely different.‍‍‍‍‍‍‍‍‍‍‍‍‍‍‍‍‍‍‍‍‍‍‍‍‍‍‍

Day One

The house Buffett has lived in for more than 30 years.‍

I picked up a few petals from the ground and pressed them in my notebook to take home. I'd like to live like Mr. Buffett, too — completely unburdened by material things.‍‍

The McDonald's and the office he tap-dances off to work to. Along the way we met lots of people who all said they know Buffett — he's like the town squire whom everyone knows and says hello to.‍‍‍‍

At the steakhouse he often goes to, there's a life-size cardboard cutout of him that they carry to each table so people can take photos. In such a small city, think how much economic benefit Buffett brings. I think his city and community is very proud of him, and he is very happy to do the contribution too.

The shareholder discount shopping event: lots of booths in a huge venue, just like a trade fair. All the US$1 DQ you can eat — remembering his investment in DQ, I broke my sugar fast.‍‍‍‍‍‍‍‍‍‍‍‍‍‍‍‍‍‍‍‍

Day Two

Not for nothing were we the most hardcore crew — we were lining up at the entrance at four in the morning. We even did a little strategic research on which section to head for and settled on sections 122/106, with a very clear target. (At 4 a.m., quite a few people were already in line.)‍‍‍‍‍‍‍

The world still rewards the diligent: we got really great seats.‍‍‍

We could almost make out the expressions on the two old gentlemen's faces, and spotting Bill Gates was no problem at all.

Here are my messy notes.

I haven't tidied or edited them, because that would take a lot of time and might mean I'd never end up posting this. So here it is as is — no responsibility taken for mistakes:‍‍‍‍‍‍‍‍‍‍‍‍‍‍‍‍‍‍‍

Value investing is a faithSome come to make a wish, some come to give thanks for one answeredSome are here for the 1st time, some for the 5th, some for the 35th. A young investor who has come five years in a row said that knowing is easy and doing is hard, so he comes to strengthen his conviction. You need to keep coming back to recharge in order to stay true to why you started. Feeling value investing in person

Doing value investing in China's A-share market: 20% of the time it's tough, 70% it's extremely tough, and only 10% of the time does it feel great. The shareholder discount day is in a huge stadium, booth after booth. From DQ ice cream to wall paint, affordable boots, clothing, bedding and insurance, all the way to yachts and private jets.

Not sexy — like a big-box store for city residents — it showed me a generation of companies that took off riding the "American Dream." People who live simply can do things that are anything but simple

There seem to be far more hotels in Omaha than in other cities. Walk into the McDonald's next to Buffett's home, and everyone from the cashier to the old gentleman eating breakfast knows Buffett, has played bridge with him, or. How hard it is for someone to remain like this after achieving such extraordinary success. Harder still, it was in exactly this manner that he succeeded.
