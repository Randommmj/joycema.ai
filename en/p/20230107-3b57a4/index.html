My 2022 learning on the "Dedao app"I've always admired Charlie Munger's broad, well-rounded general knowledge. In life and in business alike, the breadth of one's understanding is crucial. There are four "states of knowing" — not knowing what you don't know, knowing what you don't know, knowing what you know, and not knowing what you know. My goal for 2022 was to reach the state of "knowing what I don't know". Gulping things down whole doesn't let you truly master much; the depth and granularity are so rough they're close to nothing. But done this way, my map of understanding won't have any major gaps. When I run into problems and confusion, at least I know what area the problem lies in and which direction to search and study in. Translated into a study plan, this grand goal became: in 2022, work through all of Dedao's general-knowledge courses once. Once compiled, that came to 56 courses for the year, requiring 1,231 hours (73,908 minutes) — 4 hours 10 minutes a day at normal speed, or 2 hours 42 minutes a day at 1.5x speed.

III. My 2022 resolutions and how they turned out

Right, so it's time to take stock of the resolutions... Not counting work goals, I set 25 "personal resolutions" in 2022. Six were 100% completed, the vast majority were only partly done, and four were all but abandoned.

Although I fell far short of my goals, this year of learning wasn't wasted either — I really did do better than in 2021. So setting them was worthwhile, and a resolution that fell flat beats one never made. I still have to console myself — every inch forward brings an inch of joy.

At heart, none of these resolutions are goals; they are just "key results" designed to achieve the goals

That is: OKR (Objectives and Key Results)

Being able to tell "objectives and key results" apart is hugely important.

So what is my Objective?

Steady, continuous growth, and letting my teammates gain "growth and income" that match the company's development, in a "sustainable", healthy way.
