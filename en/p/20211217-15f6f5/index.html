December 17, 2021 · 10 min read

We humans come factory-set to pay extra attention to bad news — psychology has a term for it: "negativity bias." Over the long course of evolution, the humans more sensitive to risk were the ones more likely to survive. That's also why we're surrounded by anxious news every day, constantly feeling as if the world is falling apart.
