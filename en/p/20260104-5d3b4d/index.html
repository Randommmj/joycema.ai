My 10 Favorite Books of 2025

January 4, 2026 · 1 min read

Over the past year I read whatever I felt like, and a lot of it was history and philosophy.

My conclusion: I deeply agree with Durant's famous line:

Most history

is guessing,

and the rest is prejudice. It's not that those who came before us were incompetent; it's just that the "facts" we humans can take in are so limited. That's exactly why we have to read a lot of history, from all kinds of angles, if we're to have any chance of shaking off the prejudice and narrowness that follow us like a shadow.

My 10 favorite books of 2025, in no particular order:

  1. The Hitchhiker's Guide to the Galaxy

  2. Dedao Quality Control Handbook 11.0

  3. How to Know a Person

  4. The Lessons of History

  5. The State of Affairs: Rethinking Infidelity

  6. Going Global: The Checkpoints and Breakthroughs of Chinese Manufacturing's Worldwide Expedition

  7. Rockefeller's Autobiography: If You Never Concede, You'll Never Lose

  8. I Carry the Universe with Me: Poems of Fernando Pessoa

  9. Principles for Navigating Big Debt Crises

  10. Perfectly Reasonable Deviations from the Beaten Track: The Letters of Richard P. Feynman
