SFMOMA Collection Highlights, 1900 to Now

July 29, 2023

Staying in San Francisco for a little while, I went ahead and got an annual membership to SFMOMA, the San Francisco Museum of Modern Art, with the plan of bringing my laptop here every day to work.

What I never tire of is the collection exhibition from 1900 to now — every single piece is a classic.

Leaving the museum, on my way back to the hotel,

I snapped the reflections in a gallery's shop window at dusk,

and they too seemed to carry the flavour of contemporary art.
