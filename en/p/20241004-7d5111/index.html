What Is Strategy

The LEGO Crisis

October 4, 2024 · 1 min read

Most companies don’t DIE from starvation but from digestion.

Too many opportunities. -- Professor Juan

Strategy means everyone in the company understands what the overall direction is, so that they don't work against each other. It's like placing a magnet over a pile of thumbtacks pointing every which way📍

A good statement of strategy includes: objective, scope, and path.

  1. When analysing a company, you have to count in all the stakeholders / think through how a strategy affects everyone — customers, channels, employees, suppliers and so on.

  2. The opening moves of the non-family professional CEO who turned LEGO around were fascinating:

Right at the start he told the shareholders' meeting: We don’t have strategy, we only have plans of actions. We don't have a plan, but we do have a series of action plans.

In the second stage, the strategy gradually became clear. Second stage, slightly we have strategy

The strategy was — no growth. Daring to say that is impressive in itself; strategy isn't blind growth, it's finding the right direction. We plan to have no growth

  1. Diversification has to build on your strengths. Diversify have to be on your strength.

  2. Be very clear and precise about your objective. Be clear about your objective.

  3. The four elements — price, cost, customers' willingness, suppliers' willingness — need to be aligned with the objective, not pulling against each other. The four components: your price, your cost, willingness of your customers, willingness of suppliers to supply —— this four must be align to objective.
