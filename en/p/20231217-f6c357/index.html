A Brief Week Back at HBS

December 17, 2023 · 1 min read

A few memoriesThe last time I took classes at Harvard was back in 2018. Looking through the photos from then, I looked so green.

Time has settled a lot of things.

A couple of days ago, standing in the glass-roofed courtyard of the Harvard Art Museums and looking at the marble columns, I suddenly felt dazed, and my mind went straight back to five years ago.

What makes me happy is that I'm still in touch with most of my classmates from back then, and we've become important sources of support for each other.

What I took away this time

This past week, input and output ran side by side.

A new realisation about case learning and teaching at Harvard Business School: the models taught in class matter, but they're not everything. Other things that matter just as much, or even more, are... understanding what kind of manager you are; testing the dimensions and blind spots of how you think about business; sparking your passion and joy as a founder; experiencing the intellectual, political and social challenges of business; applying what you've learned to your past and future businesses.
